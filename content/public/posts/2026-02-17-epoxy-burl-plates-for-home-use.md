This is a small home build project: combining **epoxy resin** with **burl wood** to make practical plates for daily use.

I like this kind of project because it sits in the middle of craft and function — each piece is visually unique, but still useful at home.

## Build idea

- Material: burl wood + clear epoxy
- Goal: stable, easy-to-clean plates for home serving/display
- Style: keep natural wood character, let resin complete the form

## Notes from this build

1. **Wood selection matters**
   - Burl grain gives strong visual texture
   - Irregular edges can be turned into a design feature

2. **Resin balance is key**
   - Enough resin to stabilize and fill
   - Avoid overfilling and losing the wood character

3. **Home-use first**
   - Practical size
   - Smooth edges and finish
   - Built for real use, not just display
